What is understood

STIG checklist
.ckl and .cklb
 Rule, group, severity, status, CCI references, check and fix text, and the reviewer's own comments.
SCAP results
XCCDF and ARF
 Rule results joined to their definitions, with CCI and CVE identifiers taken from either the rule or the result.
Nessus and ACAS
.nessus
 Plugins with CVEs and CVSS, plus compliance audit results, which are read as configuration checks rather than vulnerabilities.
Spreadsheets
.csv
 Anything with a header row. Column headings from the common exporters are recognised automatically and shown for confirmation.

Re-importing is safe

A finding is identified by its rule and the asset it is about. Loading next month's scan updates the existing rows: the first-seen date stays, so age stays meaningful, and any decision you recorded is left alone.

A finding the scanner stops reporting closes itself, unless somebody has recorded a different decision about it.
