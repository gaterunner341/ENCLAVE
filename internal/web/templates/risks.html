What belongs here

A finding that will be patched next Tuesday does not need an entry. One that needs a waiver, a compensating control, funding, or a date somebody has to answer for does.

Entries carry control references, so an accepted risk shows up against the control it weakens rather than disappearing into a spreadsheet.
